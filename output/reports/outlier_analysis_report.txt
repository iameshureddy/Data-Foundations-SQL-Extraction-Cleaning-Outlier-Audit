METHOD DIFFERENCE EXPLANATION
----------------------------------------------------------------------
IQR is distribution-free and is generally more robust to skewed data and extreme values, whereas Z-score relies on the mean and standard deviation and is most appropriate when the distribution is approximately normal. Therefore, the two methods can identify different observations when the data are skewed or contain extreme values.
